From: Mara Lind (editor)
Re: [pacel] Accept version 0x01 frames?

Decided at the interim meeting: no. draft-01 used a different checksum, so accepting 0x01 frames
silently corrupts data. Section 3.2 in the final text requires rejecting every version except 0x02.
