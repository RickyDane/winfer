Add-ons

Burst

Raises limits by 50% for endpoints in the standard and heavy tiers (light and bulk are unaffected). Applied after the plan multiplier, rounded down.

Bulk Pack

Multiplies bulk-tier limits by 4.

Audit Plus

Keeps audit-log entries for 7 years instead of 1.
