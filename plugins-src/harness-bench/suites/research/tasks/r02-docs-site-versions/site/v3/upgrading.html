Upgrading from 2.x to 3.x

  • Client.stream() was renamed to Client.open_stream().
  • verify= became tls_verify=.
  • retries=N was replaced by retry=RetryPolicy(...).
  • HTTPError was renamed to StatusError.
  • The default timeout went from 30 to 10 seconds.
