Glass header: iterations

Five refinements of the Glass direction, each one adding to the previous. A–C focus on structure, D adds per-agent colour, and E puts everything together in a realistic 4-pane workspace with a calmer backdrop. Hover a pane to see its buttons.

AClean glass

The window dots are gone, since they're macOS chrome that doesn't do anything here. The status dot now sits on a larger, raised agent icon. Buttons are dimmed until you hover or focus the pane, and context shows as a small ring.
